# L-meta-9829 in a restricted circuit class

The sharp cost is 54 if the output is an OR of products, each product ANDs two disjunctions of signed raw pins, and all OR subexpressions may be shared. The lower bound even allows input negations for free. The statement permits arbitrary sharing and multiple overlapping stars at the same center in this class. It does not handle nested products or internal negations.

Expand any one product into the pairs consisting of one literal from each factor. Every satisfiable pair must imply an edge, because the outer OR cannot cancel an erroneous output. A pair containing a negated literal can be satisfied with at most one pin set, unless it is contradictory. Therefore all edge witnesses already occur among pairs of positive literals. Removing the negative literals, propagating constants, and trimming dead gates gives a positive circuit in the same class with no additional gates.

In a productive positive product the two factors have disjoint supports (a shared pin would accept a singleton) and their Cartesian product consists of graph edges. Since the graph has no four-cycle, one factor is a singleton. Assign that pin as the center of this product; for a single edge choose either endpoint. Write $a$ for the number of products retained in the output cone. Their centers form a vertex cover. In particular $a$ is at least the minimum vertex-cover number.

For a center $u$ let $d_u$ be the size of the union of its leaf-side supports and $a_u$ the number of products centered there. The OR network producing those $a_u$ leaf-side signals needs at least $d_u-a_u$ gates: starting with $d_u$ different pins, each binary gate can reduce the number of undirected components by at most one, and each component reaches at least one output signal. A nontrivial OR signal used for two different centers would contain two distinct common neighbors, yielding a four-cycle. Unary copies and idempotent ORs can be trimmed. Thus the preprocessing bound sums across centers. Each edge is represented at least once, so the total is at least $|E|-a$. Counting the $a$ products and at least $a-1$ output-OR gates gives $|E|+a-1$ gates or more.

Here the eleven right vertices cover every edge. A matching of size eleven certifies minimality. The exact posted statement supplies all 44 edges. The accompanying replay checks the matching, absence of four-cycles, and every one of the 16,777,216 assignments of the literal circuit. Eleven column stars attain the bound with 33 input ORs, 11 ANDs and 10 output ORs. The remaining unrestricted 53-gate problem is open in this analysis.

Restoring either missing line adds three ORs for its four points, an AND with its line pin, and one OR with the existing output. Any 53-gate witness here would therefore give a 63-gate witness for the full plane, in the same gate basis. The full-plane monotone search would specifically require a monotone witness here.
